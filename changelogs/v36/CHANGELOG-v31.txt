Service Desk v31 — Operational Intelligence Layer

Added
- Guided issue intake questions for client and agent issue creation: affected users, blocking impact, workaround, environment, start time, and business impact.
- v31 AI classification seed stored on issues for severity/category/type rationale and confidence.

Changed

- Issue detail shows guided intake, AI classification suggestion, and closure/RCA sections.
- Status update flow now updates SLA stage history consistently for normal status changes.

Notes
